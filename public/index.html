Built for food and beverage importers

If you are the U.S. owner or consignee of imported food, FDA expects you to evaluate each foreign supplier, approve it, verify it and keep the records for two years. Most small importers do this in shared folders and spreadsheets. FSVP Vault turns that into a checklist you can finish.

How it works

Set up takes an afternoon for a typical importer with a handful of suppliers.

  1. Add your suppliers and foodsAnswer five scoping questions per food. The required documents are set from the food type and its hazards.
  2. Upload what you have, request the restEach supplier gets an upload code. Expiry dates are tracked from the day a file is added.
  3. Review, approve and signYour qualified individual reviews each supplier, records the decision and signs the plan.
  4. Stay currentRenewal requests, FDA record checks and reevaluation dates keep the program up to date.

About FSVP Vault

FSVP Vault was started by a beverage importer who built an FSVP program from scratch and wanted the tool he couldn’t find: one that knows the rule, reads like a checklist, and fits a small team.

FSVP Vault is software. It organizes your records and drafts documents; it does not act as your qualified individual or as your FSVP agent, and it is not affiliated with FDA. Your qualified individual reviews and signs every decision.
