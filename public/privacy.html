Privacy policy

Last updated November 13, 2026
Draft for legal review. Questions: hello@fsvpvault.com

FSVP Vault (“we”) provides software that helps U.S. food importers keep the records required by FDA’s Foreign Supplier Verification Program (21 CFR Part 1, Subpart L). This policy explains what information we handle and how.

Information we handle

  • Account information: names, work email addresses, roles and sign-in records for people our customers invite.
  • Customer records: information our customers enter or upload about their foreign suppliers, foods, approvals, shipments and documents (for example certificates and audit reports). Our customers control this information.
  • Supplier submissions: documents, names and email addresses that a supplier sends through the upload page using a code from our customer.
  • Technical data: IP addresses and basic logs needed to run and secure the service.

How we use it

  • To provide the service: storing records, sending document requests and reminders, running checks against FDA’s public databases, and producing records packages.
  • To secure the service, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To contact account holders about their account and the service.

We don’t sell personal information, and we don’t use customer records for advertising.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that host or run the service for us (for example hosting, email delivery and code hosting), with our customer’s own users, when our customer asks us to (for example when they send a records package to FDA), or when the law requires it.

FDA public data

The service queries FDA’s public sources (import alerts, recalls, warning letters, the FDA Data Dashboard). Supplier names and registration or FEI numbers are used as search terms.

Retention

FSVP rules require importers to keep records for at least 2 years. We keep customer records while the account is active and for the period our customer chooses, then delete them. Backups are kept for a limited time.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, passwords are stored as one-way hashes, two-step sign-in is available, and each user sees only the workspaces they’re given. No system is perfectly secure; tell us right away if you suspect a problem.

Your choices

Account holders can update their details in the portal. To access, correct or delete personal information, email hello@fsvpvault.com. If your information was entered by one of our customers, we’ll work with that customer on the request.

Changes

We’ll post changes here and update the date above. Material changes will be emailed to account owners.
